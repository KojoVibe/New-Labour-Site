About the Labour Department

Serving Ghana's workforce with dedication and excellence

Our Mission

To promote decent work, protect workers' rights, ensure fair employment practices, and foster harmonious industrial relations that contribute to Ghana's socio-economic development.

Our Vision

To be a world-class labour administration that ensures decent work for all Ghanaians and promotes sustainable economic growth through effective labour market governance.

Core Values

  • Integrity: Upholding ethical standards in all our operations
  • Excellence: Delivering quality services to all stakeholders
  • Transparency: Maintaining openness in our processes and decisions
  • Inclusivity: Ensuring equal opportunities for all workers

Workers' Rights

  • Right to fair wages and equal pay
  • Right to safe working conditions
  • Right to form and join trade unions
  • Right to rest periods and annual leave
  • Protection from discrimination
  • Right to social security benefits

Employer Responsibilities

  • Provide safe working environment
  • Pay wages on time and in full
  • Comply with labour standards
  • Respect workers' rights
  • Maintain proper employment records
  • Contribute to social security schemes
